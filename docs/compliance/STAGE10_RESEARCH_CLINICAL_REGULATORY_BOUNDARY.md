# Stage 10 — Research, Clinical and Regulatory Boundary

Stage 10 prevents a technically successful genomic workflow from silently changing its intended use.

## Canonical policy

The single operational source is `config/use_boundary_policy.json`, version `1.2`, effective `2026-09-20`. The runtime gate rejects any policy identity drift and binds every release decision to the exact `case_id`, input SHA-256 and requested operation.

The software verifies externally recorded decisions. It does not decide whether OmniGenis, a module or an output is legally a medical device, clinically valid, or ethically approved for research.

## Declared-use model

Every final-release authorization carries exactly one declared class:

- nonclinical: `RESEARCH_ONLY`, `TECHNICAL_QC`, `WELLNESS_NON_CLINICAL`;
- clinical: `CLINICAL_DECISION_SUPPORT`, `CLINICAL_DIAGNOSTIC`, `MIXED_REPORT_SUITE`;
- regulatory evidence: `REGULATORY_EVIDENCE`.

A purpose or intended-use change requires a new evaluation. A prior record cannot be reused for a different case, input hash or release operation.

## Authenticated external evidence

A non-empty `evidence_ref` is never sufficient by itself. Every Stage 10 evidence reference used to authorize release must resolve to an entry in an authenticated evidence ledger. Each ledger entry is HMAC-SHA256 authenticated and binds the evidence reference to its role, `case_id`, input SHA-256, requested operation, declared use class and decision.

The HMAC key is runtime-only (`OMNIGENIS_STAGE10_EVIDENCE_HMAC_KEY`) and is never committed to the repository. Missing, malformed, tampered or differently bound evidence fails closed. Synthetic CI uses an explicitly public test-only key and non-personal fixture data; it is not production authorization material.

## Research boundary

A research-only release must explicitly deny clinical and regulatory authorization and point to the nonclinical labeling applied to the output.
When the declared research scope involves human participants, personal/sensitive data or biological material in a context subject to Brazilian human-research rules, Stage 10 requires a separate externally issued ethics assessment with status `VERIFICADO`, an authorizing decision and an evidence reference.

OmniGenis never creates that ethics decision itself. The project records current official context from Lei 14.874/2024 and Decreto 12.651/2025, while preserving the requirement to recheck the applicable framework for real use.

## Clinical boundary

A clinical or mixed-suite release requires all of the following:

- explicit clinical-use authorization;
- verified clinical-validation evidence;
- verified professional review with a responsible-professional reference;
- verified regulatory assessment with evidence;
- an assessment decision that authorizes the stated use.

The software does not infer clinical validity from successful computation, QC, a clinician-facing report, or a genomic association.

## Regulatory boundary

A `REGULATORY_EVIDENCE` release requires explicit regulatory-use authorization and a verified regulatory assessment. The accepted decision vocabulary is deliberately external: `CLEARED_FOR_STATED_USE` or `NOT_REGULATED_FOR_STATED_USE`.

These values are operator/legal-regulatory conclusions backed by evidence. They are not generated by OmniGenis.

## Runtime integration

The final report-release assembler evaluates `RESEARCH_CLINICAL_REGULATORY_BOUNDARY_GATE` before publication in addition to consent, QC, evidence, Policy Control and final-audit requirements.
If the Stage 10 policy cannot be loaded or validated, the release is blocked rather than downgraded or guessed.

## Current official-source baseline

The Brazil profile records:

- ANVISA RDC 657/2022 materials for Software as a Medical Device;
- ANVISA's current manual for medical equipment and SaMD regularization;
- Lei 14.874/2024 on research involving human beings;
- Decreto 12.651/2025 regulating that law and the national research-ethics system.

The sources are provenance, not automated legal conclusions. Applicability, classification, ethical-review requirements and regulatory status remain case-specific and update-sensitive.

Stage 10 is a technical governance control. It is not legal advice, device classification, clinical validation, research-ethics approval, registration, certification or marketing authorization.
